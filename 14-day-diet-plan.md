# 14-day diet plan

Built against the recomposition brief: high protein, carb-cycled by training day, fibre-forward, dinners lightest. Every day below passes the validator in the build prompt — protein floor, net-carb ceiling, fibre floor, per-meal protein minimum, and dinner carrying the lowest net carbs of the day.

**Daily targets by day type**

| Day type | kcal | Protein | Net carbs | Fibre |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Lift (4×/week) | 2250 | 160 g | 150–200 g | ≥35 g |
| Swim + lift | 2100 | 155 g | 115–160 g | ≥35 g |
| Rest (2×/week) | 1900 | 150 g | 80–120 g | ≥35 g |

Protein floor is 140 g on every day regardless of type, and every main meal carries at least 30 g on its own. Carbs sit on lifting days and drop on rest days. All figures are computed from an ingredient table, not estimated per dish — see the notes at the end.

## Notes

**Where the protein comes from.** Vegetarian days hitting 140 g+ is the binding constraint in this whole plan, and it does not work with regular paneer and standard oil quantities — the first build came in at 110–135 g protein with 107–145 g fat. The plan leans on low-fat paneer, thick hung or Greek curd, tofu, soya chunks, whole dals, and sprouts, with cooking oil cut to 4–7 g per dish. Soya chunks do a disproportionate amount of work at 52 g protein per 100 g dry with almost no fat.

**Fibre and low carb pull against each other**, since most fibre in Indian food rides on carb-dense staples. Resolved by routing fibre through non-starchy vegetables, whole dals and chana, sprouts, and 8–10 g of flax or chia in several dishes, while keeping rice, wheat, and millet portions small.

**Carbs are cycled, not flat.** Four lifting days sit at 170–200 g net carbs, the swim day at ~155 g, rest days at 80–120 g. A flat low-carb intake across five training days degrades session quality, and session quality is what protects lean mass in a deficit. If you want it flatter, the ceilings are one config value each.

**Non-veg appears 3 days a week**, always as an add-on to a complete vegetarian meal rather than a replacement, so no day loses its vegetarian option.

**These numbers are estimates.** They are computed from a per-100 g ingredient table in the IFCT-2017 and USDA range, but I entered those values from general knowledge rather than reading them out of IFCT directly — verify the table before trusting the totals to the gram. Cooking losses and portion drift are real; treat everything here as directional. This is a plan built to your stated brief, not clinical advice — if there is a medical reason behind the low-carb target, run it past your doctor.
