swaps:
  - for: Yellow moong dal
    use: Whole green moong, soaked 6 hours, 4 whistles
    why: If you want the whole-moong version the title suggests; nuttier and higher in fibre
  - for: Cauliflower and french beans
    use: Frozen mixed vegetables, 2 katori
    why: No chopping; add straight to the hot kadhai
  - for: Goda masala in the sabzi
    use: Plain coriander-cumin powder with a pinch of jaggery
    why: Goda masala is a Maharashtrian pantry item not everyone keeps
steps:
  - Rinse all the moong dal and put it in the pressure cooker with three times its volume of water, a pinch of turmeric and salt. Cook on high till the first whistle, then low for 2 whistles (about 8 minutes). Release naturally, then whisk the dal smooth like a varan.
  - Meanwhile cut the cauliflower into small florets and soak them 5 minutes in salted warm water to clean. Cut the beans into 1-inch pieces. Chop the onion and tomato, dice the cucumber, cube the paneer.
  - For the dal tempering, heat a third of the oil in a small pan. Mustard seeds go in first; when they finish popping, add cumin, a pinch of hing, curry leaves and a pinch of turmeric. Pour over the dal at once, stir in the chopped tomato and simmer 5 minutes until the tomato softens. Add a squeeze of lemon if you have one and switch off.
  - For the sabzi, heat the remaining oil in a kadhai on medium. Add cumin seeds, then the onion, and cook 4 minutes until soft. Add the drained florets and the beans with the spice powders (turmeric, chilli, coriander and a pinch of goda masala if you keep it) and salt. Toss to coat.
  - Add a quarter katori of water, cover and cook on low for 8 to 10 minutes, shaking the pan once or twice, until the florets are tender at the stem but still hold their shape and the water has dried off. Uncover for the last 2 minutes on medium so the edges catch a little colour.
  - Toast the paneer cubes on a hot non-stick tawa for a minute a side with a pinch of salt and chilli powder until lightly browned. Add them to the sabzi or serve them on the side.
  - Whisk the thick curd with the ground flaxseed, the diced cucumber, a pinch of salt and roasted cumin powder. Rest 5 minutes so the flax thickens the raita.
  - "Lay out the thali: dal in one katori, sabzi and paneer together, raita in another. Eat while the dal is hot; it thickens as it cools."
notes: Cauliflower overcooks quickly, so check at 8 minutes. The dal and sabzi keep 2 days chilled and both go in a tiffin; pack the flax raita separately since it thickens further overnight and may need a spoon of water.
