swaps:
  - for: Firm tofu
    use: Low-fat paneer cubes, same weight
    why: Marinate 15 minutes only, paneer takes flavour faster
  - for: Raw grated beetroot
    use: Beetroot pressure cooked 2 whistles, peeled and diced
    why: Softer and sweeter if raw beet is too earthy for you
  - for: Grill pan
    use: Airfryer at 200°C for 10 to 12 minutes, shaking once
    why: Hands-off cooking; still no extra oil
steps:
  - Press the tofu under a heavy plate for 10 minutes to drive out water, then cut it into cubes about two fingers wide; smaller cubes fall apart on the grill.
  - In a bowl, whisk the hung curd with the ground flaxseed, the dry masalas (kashmiri red chilli, coriander, cumin, garam masala, crushed kasuri methi, a pinch of turmeric, salt) and half the lemon juice. The flax thickens the marinade so it clings.
  - Turn the tofu cubes gently in the marinade until coated. Cover and rest 20 minutes at room temperature, or up to a day in the fridge.
  - While the tofu marinates, grate the beetroot and carrot and dice the cucumber. Toss them with the remaining lemon juice, salt and a pinch of black pepper. Keep chilled; the beet stains the rest a pink that is part of the look.
  - Heat a grill pan or heavy tawa on high and brush the oil over it thinly. Lay the tofu cubes down with space between them. Leave for 3 minutes until dark grill marks form, then turn with tongs and char the other sides, about 8 to 10 minutes in all. The marinade should look dry and blistered, not wet.
  - "Rest the tikka on a plate for 2 minutes. Optional: thread onto skewers and grill under an oven at 220°C for 12 minutes, turning once."
  - Pile the beetroot salad on two plates and set the tofu tikka on top. Sprinkle a pinch of chaat masala over the tikka and serve warm.
notes: Do not move the tofu early or the crust stays on the pan. Marinated tofu keeps a day in the fridge before grilling; the grilled tikka is good cold in a tiffin with the salad packed separately.
