steps:
  - Rinse all the quinoa in a strainer under running water, rubbing the grains with your fingers for a minute; this washes off the bitter coating. Drain well.
  - Put the rinsed quinoa in a saucepan with double its volume of water and a pinch of salt. Bring to a boil, then cover, drop to the lowest flame and cook for 12 to 14 minutes until the water is absorbed and each grain shows a tiny white curl. Switch off and leave covered for 5 minutes, then fluff with a fork.
  - While the quinoa cooks, heat the oil in a kadhai on medium. Add mustard seeds and let them crackle, then cumin, a pinch of hing, a broken dry red chilli and a few curry leaves.
  - Add the diced carrot and chopped beans with a pinch of salt. Cook for 4 to 5 minutes with a splash of water and the lid on, so they steam-soften without extra oil.
  - Stir in the peas and the rest of the dry masalas (turmeric, a little red chilli powder). Cook for 2 more minutes; the peas should turn bright green.
  - Add the paneer cubes and toss gently for a minute, just long enough to warm them through; over-cooking makes low-fat paneer rubbery.
  - Add the fluffed quinoa, mix lightly so the grains stay separate, and check salt. Cover and leave on low for 2 minutes so the flavours meet.
  - Switch off, scatter the chopped coriander on top and squeeze a little lemon over if you like. Rest for 2 minutes before serving.
notes: Do not skip rinsing the quinoa or the upma will taste faintly soapy. Keeps well for tiffin; it stays moist at room temperature better than rava upma does.
