    why: Guava is seasonal; any firm low-sugar fruit works
  - for: Hanging curd at home
    use: Ready Greek curd from the pack
    why: Skips the 2-hour wait
  - for: Ground flaxseed
    use: Chia seeds soaked 10 minutes in a little water
    why: Same fibre, slightly jelly texture
steps:
  - If starting from plain curd, line a strainer with a muslin cloth, pour the curd in, gather the cloth and let it drain over a bowl in the fridge for 2 to 3 hours or overnight until it is thick enough to hold a spoon upright.
  - Scrape the hung curd into a bowl and whisk it for a minute until smooth and creamy.
  - Stir the ground flaxseed into the curd along with a pinch of salt or, if you prefer it sweet, a pinch of cinnamon. Let it sit 5 minutes so the flax softens and thickens the curd slightly.
  - Wash the guavas, trim the ends and dice them into bite-size pieces, skin on. Chop the almonds roughly; if you like them crunchy, toast them on a dry tawa on low for 2 minutes, shaking the pan.
  - Divide the curd between two bowls, pile the guava on top and scatter the almonds over. A pinch of chaat masala or black pepper on the guava is optional but good.
notes: Keep the ground flaxseed in the fridge; it goes rancid quickly once ground. The curd and flax can be mixed the night before, but cut the guava fresh so it does not brown or go soft.
