swaps:

    use: Thick Greek-style curd straight from the tub
    why: Skips 2 hours of draining in muslin
  - for: Fresh methi leaves
    use: Kasuri methi, 1 tbsp crushed into the greens at the end
    why: Off-season substitute
  - for: Tawa grilling
    use: Airfryer at 200 C for 10 minutes, or oven grill 12 minutes, turning once
    why: Hands-off if you have one; the tawa is the default
steps:
  - Whisk the hung curd in a bowl with the ground flaxseed, the tandoori-style spice powders (chilli, coriander, garam masala, turmeric, a pinch of kasuri methi and chaat masala if you keep them), salt and half the lemon juice. The flax thickens the marinade so it clings instead of sliding off.
  - Cut the paneer into large cubes, the capsicum into squares and the onion into petals. Fold them into the marinade gently so the paneer does not crumble. Rest at least 30 minutes on the counter, or up to a day in the fridge.
  - Meanwhile pick the palak and methi leaves off their stems, wash in three changes of water and drain in a strainer. Chop roughly.
  - Heat a heavy tawa or grill pan on high until a drop of water dances, then brush on two-thirds of the oil. Lay the marinated paneer, capsicum and onion in a single layer. Leave them 2 minutes untouched, then turn each piece with tongs; 2 minutes more a side until the marinade chars in spots and smells smoky. Do not crowd the tawa; do it in two rounds if needed.
  - For the greens, heat the remaining oil in a kadhai on medium-high. Add cumin seeds until they crackle, a pinch of chilli, then the methi first for 1 minute, then the palak in handfuls. Toss 3 to 4 minutes until wilted and glossy but still bright green. Season with salt only at the end so the leaves do not weep.
  - Plate the greens, lay the tandoori paneer and vegetables over them, squeeze the remaining lemon on top and rest 2 minutes before eating.
notes: Low-fat paneer dries out fast, so pull it off the heat as soon as it colours. Marinate in the morning for a 20-minute dinner. Grilled paneer reheats poorly, so grill just before eating; the marinated raw cubes keep a day.
