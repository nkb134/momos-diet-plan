steps:
  - Boil all the soya chunks in salted water for 5 minutes, drain, rinse cold and squeeze out the water. Halve them so more surface can brown.
  - "Cut the vegetables while the soya boils: shred the cabbage, slice the capsicum into strips, cut the carrot into thin matchsticks. Keep them in separate piles because they go in at different times."
  - Heat the oil in a wide kadhai on high until it just starts to smoke. Add the ginger-garlic paste and stir for 20 seconds; do not let it burn.
  - Add the squeezed soya and spread it out in one layer. Leave it for a minute without stirring so the undersides brown, then toss and repeat for 3 to 4 minutes until the chunks have golden edges.
  - Add the carrot sticks and toss for 2 minutes, then the capsicum for another minute. Keep the flame high and the pan moving; you want scorched edges, not steamed vegetables.
  - Add the shredded cabbage with the dry masalas (crushed black pepper, red chilli flakes, salt, a pinch of garam masala if you like). Toss for 2 minutes only; the cabbage should wilt slightly but still crunch. Splash a spoon of water if anything sticks. Switch off.
  - Whisk the curd with a pinch of salt and stir the ground flaxseed into it. Serve the hot stir-fry in a bowl with the flax curd spooned on the side for dipping or mixing through.
notes: A hot pan and small batches are what stop the vegetables from going soggy; if your kadhai is small, stir-fry in two rounds. Keeps 2 days chilled; eat cold as a salad or reheat quickly on high.
