steps:
  - Cut the paneer into bite-sized cubes. Toss them with a pinch of salt, turmeric, and a generous amount of coarsely crushed black pepper from the spice mix; pepper is the main flavour here. Let them sit 5 minutes.
  - Dry-roast the peanuts in a small kadhai on medium for 3 to 4 minutes, shaking the pan, until the skins crack and they smell toasty. Tip onto a plate to cool and crisp; crush them roughly if you like.
  - Dice the cucumber and capsicum small so they mix through the bowl.
  - Whisk the thick curd with the ground flaxseed, a pinch of salt, a pinch of roasted cumin and a pinch of chilli. Rest it 5 minutes; the flax swells and makes a spoonable dressing.
  - Heat a non-stick tawa on medium-high and brush on all the oil. Lay the paneer cubes in a single layer and cook 1.5 to 2 minutes a side until the pepper crust darkens and the surface turns golden. Do not shift them early.
  - "Build the bowls: cucumber and capsicum at the bottom, the hot peppered paneer on top, the flax curd spooned over the side, and the roasted peanuts scattered last so they stay crunchy. Curry leaves crisped on the tawa for a few seconds make a good garnish."
notes: Add the peanuts just before eating. For tiffin, pack the flax curd separately and keep the cucumber away from the paneer so nothing goes soggy; the paneer is fine cold.
