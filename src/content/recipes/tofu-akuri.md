steps:
  - "Make the bajra dough first: put the flour in a bowl with a pinch of salt and add hot (not boiling) water a spoon at a time, mixing with a spoon and then kneading with your palm for 4 to 5 minutes until it turns smooth and holds together. Bajra has no gluten, so the kneading matters. Cover and rest 10 minutes."
  - Press the tofu between two plates for 10 minutes to squeeze out water, then crumble it roughly with your fingers into pieces the size of a pea and smaller.
  - Divide the dough into two balls; the flour makes two rotis, one per person. Pat each into a round on a sheet of plastic or between two greased plastic sheets with a rolling pin, working from the centre and patching cracks with a wet finger.
  - Heat the tawa on medium-high. Peel a roti off the plastic onto your palm and lay it on the tawa. Cook for about a minute until the top dries, flip, and cook until brown spots appear. Flip once more and press with a cloth to puff. Sprinkle a few drops of water on the tawa before the first side if the roti looks like it might crack. Keep wrapped in a cloth.
  - For the akuri, heat the oil in the kadhai on medium. Add cumin seeds; when they darken, add the chopped onion and green chilli and cook 4 minutes until soft and just golden.
  - Add the tomato with the dry masalas (turmeric, red chilli, a little coriander powder, a pinch of garam masala, salt) and cook 3 minutes until it breaks down.
  - Add the crumbled tofu and stir on medium for 4 to 5 minutes until it turns yellow all over and any water it gives out has cooked off. It should look like soft scrambled eggs, still moist, not dry.
  - Switch off, stir in the ground flaxseed and the chopped coriander, and rest a minute. Serve the akuri hot with the bajra roti.
notes: Bajra roti goes hard as it cools, so make it last and eat it warm; wrap in a cloth if there is a gap. The akuri reheats well and can be spooned into a tiffin box with a wheat roti instead.
