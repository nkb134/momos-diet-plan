steps:
  - Rinse all the sprouts in a strainer and pick out any unsprouted hard beans. Put them in a steamer or a strainer set over a pot of boiling water, cover, and steam for 5 to 6 minutes. They should be tender enough to bite cleanly but still hold their shape; overcooked sprouts turn mealy.
  - Spread the steamed sprouts on a plate to cool for 5 minutes; mixing them hot will wilt the onion and tomato.
  - Chop the onion and tomato fine while the sprouts cool. Whisk the curd in a bowl with a pinch of salt and roasted cumin until smooth and pourable.
  - In a wide bowl, toss the cooled sprouts with the onion, tomato, most of the coriander, and the dry masalas (chaat masala, roasted cumin, a pinch of red chilli, black salt). Squeeze the lemon over and mix.
  - "Taste and adjust: it should be tangy and a little sharp, because the curd will soften it."
  - Spoon the sprouts into two bowls, pour the seasoned curd over the top like a dahi chaat, and finish with the remaining coriander and a last pinch of chaat masala.
notes: Sprouts and curd separately keep 2 days in the fridge; assemble just before eating so the sprouts stay crisp. For tiffin, carry the curd in its own box and pour over at the table.
