  - for: Cucumber kachumber

    use: A spoon of curd or a splash of vinegar
    why: No lemon in the house
steps:
  - Chop half the onion and one tomato finely for the bhurji, along with the capsicum. Crumble the paneer with your fingers into small, uneven bits.
  - For the kachumber, slice the rest of the onion thin, dice the other tomato and the cucumber, and toss them in a bowl with a pinch of salt, a squeeze of lemon, a pinch of chaat masala or roasted cumin from the mix, and half the coriander. Leave it in the fridge while you cook; add the salt only at the last minute if you want it crunchy.
  - Heat all the oil in a non-stick kadhai on medium. Add cumin seeds; when they sizzle, add the chopped onion with a pinch of salt and cook 3 to 4 minutes until translucent, adding a spoon of water if it sticks.
  - Add the capsicum and cook 2 minutes, then the tomato and the spice powders (turmeric, chilli, coriander). Cook 3 minutes until the tomato softens and the masala thickens.
  - Add the crumbled paneer and salt and fold gently for 2 to 3 minutes on medium, just until hot and evenly coloured. Overcooking makes low-fat paneer chewy.
  - Switch off, scatter the remaining coriander over the bhurji, and serve straight away with the chilled kachumber on the side.
notes: Keep the kachumber unsalted until serving or it turns watery. The bhurji keeps a day chilled; the salad does not, so cut it fresh. This is a light dinner on its own; add a roti if the day was long.
