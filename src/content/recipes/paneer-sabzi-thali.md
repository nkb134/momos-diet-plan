swaps:
  - for: Fresh bhindi
    use: Frozen cut bhindi, cooked from frozen on high heat
    why: Saves washing and drying, works off-season
  - for: Lauki
    use: Tinda, pumpkin or tori, same weight
    why: Any soft gourd suits the tomato sabzi
  - for: Two separate sabzis
    use: "One mixed sabzi: brown the bhindi, then add the lauki and tomato to the same kadhai"
    why: One pan, 10 minutes less
steps:
  - Wash the bhindi first and spread it on a cloth to dry fully before cutting; wet bhindi turns slimy. Peel the lauki and cut into thumb-sized cubes. Chop the onion and tomato, grate the cucumber, cube the paneer.
  - "Make the jowar dough: flour, a pinch of salt, hot water added a spoon at a time, kneaded 3 to 4 minutes until smooth. Cover with a damp cloth."
  - "Bhindi first: heat a little over half the oil in a wide non-stick kadhai on medium-high. Trim the dried bhindi, cut into 1-inch pieces and add in one layer. Leave 3 minutes before the first stir, then stir only every couple of minutes, lid off, for 8 to 10 minutes until the pieces have browned edges and no stringiness. Add turmeric, chilli, coriander powder, amchur if you keep it, and salt only at the end. Tip onto a plate."
  - "Lauki next: in a pan, heat the remaining oil on medium, add cumin seeds until they crackle, then the onion and cook 4 minutes until soft. Add the tomato and the rest of the spice powders, cook 3 minutes, then the lauki cubes, salt and a quarter katori of water. Cover and simmer on low 10 to 12 minutes until the lauki is translucent and tender."
  - Toast the paneer cubes on a hot dry non-stick tawa for a minute a side with a pinch of salt and chilli powder until lightly browned. Fold half into the lauki sabzi and serve the rest on the side.
  - "Raita: whisk the thick curd with the ground flaxseed, the grated cucumber (squeezed lightly), a pinch of salt and roasted cumin powder. Rest 5 minutes so the flax thickens it."
  - "Rotis last: divide the dough into 2 balls, pat each with wet palms into a round the thickness of a coin, cook on a hot tawa 1 minute, flip when spotted, 1 minute more, then puff over the flame with tongs."
  - "Set out the thali: bhindi, lauki with paneer, raita and one jowar roti each. Eat the roti while it is warm."
notes: Cook the bhindi uncovered and salt it late to keep it dry. Both sabzis keep a day chilled and travel well in a tiffin; pack the flax raita separately. Jowar rotis go stiff as they cool, so wrap them in a cloth if carrying.
