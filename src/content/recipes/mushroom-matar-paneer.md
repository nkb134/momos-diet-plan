swaps:
  - for: Button mushrooms
    use: Cauliflower florets or lauki cubes, same weight
    why: Mushrooms are not always fresh at the local sabziwala
  - for: Blended onion-tomato gravy
    use: "Skip the mixie: cook the onion and tomato longer and mash with the ladle for a rustic gravy"
    why: Fewer dishes; texture is chunkier but the flavour is the same
  - for: Fresh rotis
    use: Two leftover rotis or a slice of whole-wheat bread
    why: Saves rolling time
steps:
  - Knead the atta with a pinch of salt and enough water for a soft dough; cover and rest 15 minutes. Wipe the mushrooms with a damp cloth rather than washing them, trim the stems and quarter them. Chop the onion and tomato and cube the paneer.
  - Heat half the oil in the kadhai on high. Add the mushrooms in one layer and leave them 2 minutes before stirring; they should brown rather than sweat. Cook 5 minutes until they shrink and their liquid has evaporated, then lift out onto a plate.
  - Lower the heat to medium, add the remaining oil, and put in cumin seeds; when they darken and sizzle, add the onion with a pinch of salt. Cook 5 minutes with a splash of water until soft and light gold. Add the tomato and the spice powders (turmeric, coriander, chilli, a pinch of garam masala) and cook 5 more minutes until the tomato collapses and the mixture looks glossy.
  - Let the masala cool 2 minutes, blend it in the mixie with a quarter katori of water to a smooth paste, and return it to the kadhai. Simmer 3 minutes; it should thicken and the raw tomato smell should be gone.
  - Add the peas and half a katori of hot water, cover and simmer 5 minutes until the peas are tender. Return the mushrooms and add the paneer cubes, stir gently, cover and cook 3 minutes on low so the paneer absorbs the gravy. Check salt and switch off. Rest 5 minutes with the lid on.
  - "Divide the dough into 4 balls and roll each thin. Cook on a hot tawa: 30 seconds until bubbles show, flip, 45 seconds until spotted, then flip once more and press the edges with a cloth or tongs so it puffs. No ghee needed."
  - Serve the mushroom matar paneer with the rotis and the thick curd in a separate katori, whisked with a pinch of roasted cumin if you like.
notes: Do not crowd the mushrooms or they steam grey instead of browning. The curry keeps 2 days chilled and reheats well; for tiffin, pack the rotis wrapped in a cloth and the curry in a leak-proof box.
