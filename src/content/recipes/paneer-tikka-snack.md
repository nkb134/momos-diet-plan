  - for: Tawa

    why: Hands-off if you own one
steps:
  - If using wooden skewers, soak them in water for 15 minutes so they do not scorch on the tawa.
  - Whisk the hung curd with the spice powders (chilli, coriander, garam masala, turmeric, a pinch of chaat masala and kasuri methi if you keep them), salt and half the lemon juice. It should be thick enough to hold a spoon upright.
  - Cut the paneer into large cubes, the capsicum into squares and the onion into petals. Fold them into the marinade gently and rest 20 to 30 minutes on the counter, or overnight in the fridge.
  - Thread onto skewers, alternating paneer, capsicum and onion, leaving a little space between pieces so the heat reaches all sides.
  - Heat a heavy tawa or grill pan on high until a drop of water dances across it, then brush on all the oil. Lay the skewers down and leave them 2 minutes untouched. Turn with tongs and cook 2 minutes on each remaining side until the marinade chars in spots and smells smoky, about 8 minutes in all.
  - Squeeze the rest of the lemon over the hot skewers, sprinkle a pinch of chaat masala, and rest 2 minutes before eating so the paneer relaxes.
notes: Low-fat paneer dries out quickly, so pull the skewers as soon as they colour. Marinate in the morning for a 10-minute evening snack. The cubes travel fine cold in a tiffin, but grill them the same day.
