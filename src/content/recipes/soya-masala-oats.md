steps:
  - Pulse the soya chunks in the mixie a few times to break them into coarse granules (skip if you bought granules). Soak them in a bowl of hot salted water for 10 minutes, then drain in a strainer and press out the water with the back of a spoon.
  - Dry toast the oats in a kadhai on medium for 2 to 3 minutes until they smell nutty; this keeps them from turning gluey. Tip them out and keep aside.
  - Heat the oil in the same kadhai. Add cumin seeds; when they sizzle, add the chopped onion and a slit green chilli. Cook 3 to 4 minutes until soft.
  - Add the diced capsicum and chopped tomato with the dry masalas (turmeric, red chilli, a pinch of garam masala, black pepper, salt). Cook 3 minutes until the tomato collapses, splashing in water if it sticks.
  - Stir in the squeezed soya granules and cook for 2 minutes so they soak up the masala.
  - Add the toasted oats and about 2 glasses of hot water. Stir, bring to a bubble, then cook uncovered on medium-low for 4 to 5 minutes, stirring now and then, until the oats are soft and the mixture is thick but still spoonable. The capsicum should keep a little bite.
  - Switch off and rest 2 minutes; it thickens as it stands. Serve hot with the curd on the side, or stir a spoonful through each bowl for a creamier finish.
notes: Masala oats sets hard if left, so add hot water and stir when reheating. Not the best tiffin candidate; eat it fresh in the morning.
