    why: Hands-off and no smoke, if you have one

    use: Grated ginger with a squeeze more lemon
    why: No-garlic version
steps:
  - Rinse the fish and pat it very dry with a cloth; a wet surface will not crisp. Make two shallow slashes on each thick piece.
  - In a bowl mix the ginger-garlic paste, red chilli powder, turmeric, crushed black pepper, salt, half the lemon juice and a few curry leaves torn small. Rub this into the fish, working it into the slashes, and leave it for 20 minutes at room temperature.
  - Heat a non-stick tawa or grill pan on medium-high for 2 full minutes until a drop of water skips across it. Brush the oil onto the fish rather than the pan.
  - Lay the fish down and do not move it for 4 minutes; the marinade will set into a crust and the piece will release on its own when it is ready. If it sticks, give it another 30 seconds.
  - Turn it once with tongs and cook the second side 3 to 4 minutes more, until the flesh at the thickest part is opaque and flakes under gentle pressure. Skin side, if any, goes down last so it crisps.
  - Rest the fish for 2 minutes on the plate and squeeze the remaining lemon over it.
  - "This is a non-veg add-on: grill it on its own tawa and serve it beside the vegetarian dinner."
notes: Thick steaks may need a minute more per side; do not press them down or they dry out. Grilled fish is best straight off the tawa and does not pack well, so make it fresh.
