steps:
  - "Soak all the brown rice for 30 minutes, drain, and pressure cook with 2 times its volume of water and a pinch of salt: high flame to the first whistle, then low for 15 minutes. Let the pressure release naturally and leave the lid on until serving."
  - Boil the soya chunks in salted water for 5 minutes until swollen and soft. Drain, rinse in cold water and squeeze hard to remove the water. Halve any large pieces.
  - Heat the oil in a kadhai on medium. Add cumin seeds; once they sizzle, add the chopped onion and cook 6 to 7 minutes until golden, adding a spoon of water whenever it threatens to stick.
  - Add the dry masalas (turmeric, coriander powder, red chilli, a little garam masala, salt) and stir for 20 seconds, then pour in the tomato puree. Cook, stirring, for 5 to 6 minutes until the masala darkens, thickens and smells sweet rather than raw.
  - Add the squeezed soya chunks and toss for 2 minutes so they pick up the masala. Add the peas and a glass of hot water, cover, and simmer on low for 10 minutes. The soya should be plump and the gravy thick enough to cling to it.
  - For the raita, whisk the curd with a pinch of salt and roasted cumin until smooth, then fold in the grated cucumber. Chill until needed.
  - Rest the curry off the heat for 5 minutes with a sprinkle of coriander if you have it. Fluff the rice with a fork and serve with the soya curry over it and the raita on the side.
notes: The curry keeps 2 days in the fridge and reheats well with a splash of water; soya actually absorbs more flavour overnight. Pack raita separately in a leak-proof box for tiffin.
