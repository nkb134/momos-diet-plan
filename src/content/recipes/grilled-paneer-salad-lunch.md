swaps:
  - for: Button mushrooms
    use: Zucchini or capsicum strips, dry-seared the same way
    why: Mushrooms are not always fresh at the vegetable cart
  - for: Chia seeds
    use: Ground flaxseed, same amount
    why: Either seed thickens the curd dressing and adds fibre
  - for: Wilting the palak
    use: Baby palak leaves used raw
    why: One less step if the leaves are tender
steps:
  - Stir the chia seeds into the curd with two spoons of water and set it aside for 10 minutes to swell. Meanwhile wash the palak, shred the cabbage, grate the carrot and beetroot, dice the cucumber and slice the mushrooms. Cut the paneer into slabs about 1.5 cm thick.
  - Rub the paneer slabs with half the spice mix, a pinch of salt, half the lemon juice and a teaspoon of the oil. Let them sit while you cook the mushrooms.
  - Heat a dry non-stick kadhai on high. Add the mushrooms in a single layer without oil and leave them for 2 minutes untouched, then toss; they will release water and then brown as it dries off, about 5 minutes total. Season with salt and pepper and tip them into a bowl.
  - In the same hot kadhai, add the palak with a pinch of salt and toss for a minute until it just wilts and turns glossy. Tip it out beside the mushrooms.
  - Heat a tawa or grill pan on medium-high. Lay the paneer slabs down and grill 2 to 3 minutes per side until they carry golden marks and the edges brown; turn once with tongs. Rest them a minute and cut into strips.
  - Toast the sesame seeds on the same tawa for a minute until they pop and smell nutty, then take them off before they darken.
  - Whisk the chia curd with the remaining lemon juice, the remaining oil, the rest of the spices, salt and a little black pepper into a thick dressing.
  - In a wide bowl toss the cabbage, carrot, cucumber, beetroot, palak and mushrooms with most of the dressing. Divide between two plates, lay the paneer strips on top, spoon the rest of the dressing over and scatter the toasted sesame.
notes: Grate the beetroot last and keep it slightly separate until you toss, or the whole salad turns pink. For a tiffin, pack the dressing in a small box and toss just before eating so the vegetables stay crisp.
