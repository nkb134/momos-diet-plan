steps:
  - Wrap the tofu in a clean cloth and press it under a heavy plate for 10 minutes; this pulls out water so it browns instead of steaming. Cut into small cubes and toss with a pinch of salt and pepper.
  - Toast the sesame seeds in a dry pan on low for 2 to 3 minutes, shaking, until they turn pale gold and start to jump. Tip out immediately; they burn fast.
  - Heat the oil on a non-stick tawa on medium-high, spreading it thin. Lay the tofu cubes down in a single layer and leave them for 2 minutes without moving so a golden crust forms, then turn with tongs and brown the other sides, about 5 to 6 minutes in all. Rest on a plate.
  - While the tofu cooks, slice the cucumber into thin half-moons, shred the cabbage fine, and cut the carrot into matchsticks or grate it coarsely. Salt the cabbage lightly and squeeze it with your fingers for a minute to soften it.
  - For the dressing, whisk the lemon juice with the ground flaxseed, the dry masalas (black pepper, red chilli flakes, salt), a pinch of sugar if you like, and a spoon of water so it is pourable. It thickens as the flax swells.
  - Toss the cucumber, cabbage and carrot with the dressing in a wide bowl. Add the warm tofu, toss once gently, and scatter the toasted sesame on top. Serve straight away while the tofu is still warm against the cold vegetables.
notes: Dressed cabbage weeps if it sits, so if making ahead keep the vegetables, tofu and dressing in three boxes and combine at the table. The seared tofu is fine cold and keeps 2 days chilled.
