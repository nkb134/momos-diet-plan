swaps:
  - for: Firm tofu
    use: Low-fat paneer, same weight, skip the pressing
    why: Tofu is not stocked everywhere; paneer takes the same stir-fry
  - for: Ginger-garlic paste
    use: Grated ginger only
    why: Makes the dish no-garlic for fasting days
  - for: Blending the soup in a mixie
    use: Whisk the cooked dal hard with the ladle
    why: One less thing to wash
steps:
  - Take the tofu out of its water, wrap it in a muslin cloth or clean kitchen towel and set a plate with a katori of water on top for 10 minutes. Pressed tofu browns instead of steaming. Cut it into 1-inch cubes and toss with a pinch of salt, turmeric and chilli powder.
  - Rinse all the moong dal and put it in the pressure cooker with four times its volume of water, half the ginger-garlic paste, a pinch of turmeric and pepper. Cook on high till the first whistle, then low for 2 whistles. Release naturally.
  - Blend the cooked dal in the mixie (let it cool a few minutes first, and fill the jar only halfway) or whisk it smooth. Return to the cooker, thin with hot water to a drinking consistency, add salt and a squeeze of lemon, and keep on the lowest flame while you make the stir-fry.
  - Shred the cabbage finely, cut the carrot into matchsticks and the beans on the diagonal so everything cooks in the same time.
  - Heat a non-stick kadhai on high until it smokes faintly. Add half the oil, then the tofu cubes in a single layer. Leave them 2 minutes without stirring, then turn each side once so they colour golden all over. Lift out onto a plate.
  - Add the remaining oil to the same kadhai with the rest of the ginger-garlic paste; stir 20 seconds until fragrant. Add the carrot and beans first, toss 2 minutes, then the cabbage. Keep the flame high and keep tossing so the vegetables char slightly at the edges but stay crunchy, about 3 minutes.
  - Return the tofu to the kadhai with the remaining spice powders, salt and a spoon of water to loosen anything stuck to the base. Toss 1 minute and switch off.
  - Ladle the hot soup into bowls with a little crushed pepper on top and serve the tofu stir-fry in a separate plate alongside.
notes: Keep the stir-fry moving on high heat; the moment the flame drops the cabbage releases water and goes soft. The soup keeps 2 days in the fridge and thickens, so loosen with water when reheating.
